About the hospital

Caring for Gorakhpur since 2008.

New Udai Medical Centre Pvt. Ltd. has served patients from Gandhi Gali in Golghar, the central area of Gorakhpur, since 2008 — easily reached from anywhere in the city and from the districts around it.

What began as a small clinic is today a full multi-speciality hospital — with consulting rooms, a modern operation theatre, ICU and NICU, in-patient beds and a diagnostic centre, all in one building. You can see your specialist, have your scan done and collect your report in a single visit.

An emergency team and an ambulance are available at every hour of the day and night. For planned surgery, we tell you and your family in advance what to expect — when admission is, how long the procedure takes, and when you can expect to go home.

We are empanelled for cashless treatment under most health insurance policies, and our insurance desk will help you with the paperwork.

Treatments & procedures

Complete care, from diagnosis to recovery.

From your first consultation to going home safely — surgery, maternity care, intensive care, diagnostics and emergency services, all provided here.

OrthopaedicsJoint replacement & fracture care

Knee and hip replacement, fracture treatment and keyhole joint surgery, with guidance on physiotherapy to help you get back on your feet.

Gynaecology & ObstetricsDeliveries & newborn care

Care through every stage of pregnancy, safe normal and caesarean deliveries, and a neonatal unit for babies who need extra warmth and monitoring.

General & laparoscopic surgeryPlanned & emergency operations

Appendix, gallbladder, hernia, piles, fissure and fistula, performed by keyhole surgery wherever possible — which usually means less pain and a shorter stay.

Critical careIntensive care & emergency admission

Fully monitored ICU and HDU beds for patients who need close observation, with doctors and nursing staff present through the night.

DiagnosticsImaging & pathology, on site

X-ray, ultrasound and a pathology laboratory within the hospital, so your tests and reports are usually completed during the same visit.

Endoscopy & ENTScopes, sinus and airway work

Endoscopy and colonoscopy for stomach and digestive complaints, alongside treatment for ear, nose and throat problems including tonsil surgery.
